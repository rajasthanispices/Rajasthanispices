Company Profile

Rajasthan Trading & Manufactures (RTM)

Established in 2007, RTM is based in Bhawani Mandi, Jhalawar, Rajasthan. We manufacture, trade and supply quality spices, food products and agro commodities to domestic and international markets, with a focus on consistency, timely supply and long-term business relationships.
