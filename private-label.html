If you want to sell pure spices under your own brand name, we provide complete processing, grinding, and packing facilities directly from Bhawani Mandi, Rajasthan.

HOW IT WORKS

Simple 5-Step Process

From spice quality selection to final order delivery.

01

Quality & Variety Selection

Select spice variety, required color, pungency level, and sample approval.

02

Packaging Finalization

Choose pouch or box sizes (50g to 1kg) and finalize your brand artwork.

03

Cleaning & Cold Grinding

Machine cleaning, de-stoning, and fresh grinding at controlled temperatures.

04

Pouch Packing & Sealing

Accurate machine weighing, automatic pouch filling, and clean airtight sealing.

05

Packing & Dispatch

Carton packaging and safe transport dispatch directly to your business location.
